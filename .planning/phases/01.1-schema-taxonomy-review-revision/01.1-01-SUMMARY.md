## Decisions Made
- LocationRef placed after BBox, before VerificationRecord (imports nothing from labels, pure Pydantic model)
- char_offset and char_length are Optional[int] with None defaults -- not all references need character-level precision
- NOTE_AREA and NOTE_CONTINUATION replace FOOTNOTE_AREA, ENDNOTE_AREA, FOOTNOTE_CONTINUATION -- notes are unified at spatial level
- ReferenceSystem shared by both Citation and MarginalReference, eliminating the old MarginalRefType duplication
- DocumentType renamed to DocumentSectionType to prevent collision with DocumentSource.document_type Literal field
- MIN_SUPPORTED_VERSION set to 2.0.0 -- old GT files need migration (breaking change by design)
